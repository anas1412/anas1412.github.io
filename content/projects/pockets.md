Type your monthly income and every number updates as you type. Five pockets:
essentials 55%, long-term wealth 15%, short-term goals 15%, guilt-free spending
5%, debt and investing 10%.

## Why

Budgeting apps want an account, your bank login and a subscription. This is one
page that never leaves your browser — no sign-up, no tracking.
