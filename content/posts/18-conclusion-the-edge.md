This series started with a trading definition, so it ends with one.

An edge is a small advantage that only exists across many repetitions. You
cannot feel it on any given day. A single trade tells you nothing. A losing
week tells you almost nothing. The edge only becomes visible once you have
done the same thing, the same way, enough times for luck to wash out.

That is the part people cannot bear, and it is why most of them never find
out whether they had one.

## Every idea here is that kind of edge

None of the notes in this series will change your week. Each of them, applied
for years, is the difference.

- **[[02-the-man-and-his-horse-an-analogy-for-mind-and-body|The horse]]**: knowing the rule is worthless until the habit carries it.
- **[[03-shadow-work-making-the-unconscious-conscious|The shadow]]**: the pattern you refuse to see is the one that runs you.
- **[[04-the-have-do-be-model-the-illusion-of-external-success|Have-Do-Be]]** and **[[05-the-do-have-be-model-the-treadmill-of-action|Do-Have-Be]]**: both put who you are last.
- **[[06-the-power-of-self-image-who-you-believe-you-are|Self-image]]**: you drift back to who you believe you are.
- **[[07-the-dunning-kruger-effect-the-peak-of-mount-stupid|Mount Stupid]]**: confidence is a bad measure of competence.
- **[[08-the-energetic-blueprint-frequencies-mindsets-and-manifestati|The weather inside]]**: scarcity narrows what you can see.
- **[[09-emotional-intelligence-navigating-your-inner-world|Emotional control]]**: build the pause before you need it.
- **[[10-the-state-of-high-performance-energy-clarity-and-focus|Energy, clarity, focus]]**: quality is set up in advance.
- **[[11-the-engine-of-progress-discipline-consistency-and-reflection|Discipline and the journal]]**: repeat until it lives in the horse, and write down what happened.
- **[[12-the-great-battle-delayed-vs-instant-gratification|Now or later]]**: design the environment instead of fighting it.
- **[[13-thinking-paradigms-process-probability-and-beyond-dualism|Probabilities]]**: judge the decision, not the outcome.
- **[[14-navigating-reality-the-power-of-mental-models|Better maps]]**: carry more than one.
- **[[15-embracing-the-unfair-game-paradox-and-contrarian-thinking|The unfair game]]**: ask what now, not why.
- **[[16-the-be-do-have-model-attracting-your-reality|Be-Do-Have]]**: start with who you are.
- **[[17-the-symphony-of-success-harmonizing-internal-fulfillment-and|Fulfilment]]**: stop waiting to arrive before you are allowed to feel it.

## The other edge

There is a second meaning, and it is the one in the title. The edge is also
the boundary of what you currently know and are comfortable with. Nothing in
this list works from the middle of your comfort. All of it asks you to stand
at the edge a little longer than feels natural, and do the uncomfortable
thing again.

Those two meanings turn out to be one. You find out whether you have an edge
by standing at the edge, repeatedly, long after the novelty has gone and
before the results have come.

That is the whole practice. Pick the road. Train the horse. Keep going long
enough to find out.
